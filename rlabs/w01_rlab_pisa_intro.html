About PISA

This file is a cleaned subset that includes three variables for U.S. students.

Variable What it is
PAREDINT Highest parental education in years of schooling; takes the values 3, 6, 9, 12, 14.5, 16
READ_AVG Effectively the reading literacy score (technically the mean of the plausible values)
BOOKSHOME Books in the home, an ordered 1–5 Likert-style response

See Pisa Codebook for details on what each variable

library(tidyverse)
