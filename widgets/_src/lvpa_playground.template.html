Our hypothesis: growth mindset (gm) predicts stress resistance (Stress) and math anxiety (Anx); stress resistance predicts math anxiety; and math anxiety predicts the math score (math). In lavaan: Stress ~ gm, Anx ~ gm + Stress, math ~ Anx. The data are PISA 2022 (UK): 4 growth mindset items, 10 stress resistance items, 6 math anxiety items, and the average math score (MATH_AVE). MATH_AVE is the only indicator of math, so its loading is fixed to 1 and its residual variance to 0. Every other factor is scaled by fixing its first loading to 1 (marker indicator). Every possible parameter starts out free (blue). Click a path in the diagram, or a checkbox in a matrix, to fix it to 0. The worksheet is at the bottom of this page: Worksheet.

Parameter matrices

Λ (p × m = 21 × 4): loadings of indicators on factors
B (m × m = 4 × 4): regressions between factors

Row = outcome, column = predictor. B holds the structural paths. These are not covariances.

Ψ (m × m = 4 × 4): factor variance and disturbance (co)variances

Only gm is exogenous; its variance is ψ11. The other three diagonal cells are the variances of the disturbances (the part of each factor the predictors do not explain). Off-diagonal cells are fixed to 0.

Θ (p × p = 21 × 21): residual variances and covariances of the indicators

The model-implied covariance matrix is Σ = Λ(I − B)−1 Ψ (I − B)−1′ Λ′ + Θ. Here (I − B)−1 turns the structural paths into total effects.

No estimates for this specification. A specification is only estimated here when it is identified (df ≥ 0) and matches one of the models we ran:

  • the hypothesis model: loadings only on their own factor, no residual covariances, all four structural paths free (Q4), or
  • the same model without the gm → Stress path (Q4, then also Q3).

Estimated with maximum likelihood in lavaan (marker indicators, standardized estimates shown). PISA rotates questionnaire items, so no student answers all 21 variables. The means and covariances come from an EM (FIML-equivalent) estimate using all 11,037 students, and the model was fit to that matrix, so the chi-square and p-values are approximate.

Guidelines used here: CFI and TLI ≥ .95, RMSEA ≤ .06, SRMR ≤ .08. The chi-square test is nearly always significant with N this large, so it is reported but not used alone.

Worksheet

For each question, start from the original model (click Reset: all free), make the change in the playground above, and fill in that question's column of the table. Each question starts over from the original model.

  1. Q1. Remove all residual covariances.
  2. Q2. Update the model so that each indicator is predicted only by the latent variable that makes sense for it.
  3. Q3. Drop the relationship between growth mindset and stress (remove the path from gm to Stress).
  4. Q4. Create a logically specified model.
  5. Q5. Explain how we use the information above to determine if our hypothesis about the data is good, both the measurement model and the structural model. Write your answer in the box below the table.
